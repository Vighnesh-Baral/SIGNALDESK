THE CHALLENGE

Customer feedback is everywhere.
Clarity is harder to find.

Support tickets, interviews, reviews and spreadsheets hold valuable signals. But fragmented sources make it difficult to understand recurring issues, verify what is happening and agree on what to do next.

01 / FRAGMENTED SIGNALS

Feedback lives in silos.

Important customer context is spread across exports, documents, conversations and disconnected tools.

02 / WEAK TRACEABILITY

Summaries lose the evidence.

Teams need to move from a broad theme back to the original words, source and context.

03 / UNCLEAR NEXT STEPS

Knowing the issue is only step one.

Product teams still need to validate hypotheses, compare signals and decide what to investigate.

EVIDENCE OVER ASSUMPTION

Every conclusion should have somewhere to point.

Counts are calculated from submitted records. Original feedback stays connected to themes and investigation cards. AI-generated explanations and root-cause hypotheses are labelled for review rather than presented as verified facts.

Where the evidence is incomplete or contradictory, SIGNALDESK should make that visible.
